Privacy policy

Snow Tracks (Pegadas na Neve) · last updated: November 12, 2026

Who we are

Snow Tracks (Pegadas na Neve in Portuguese) is made by Tomás Mota, in Portugal, who is the controller of the data described here. Contact: tmota474@gmail.com.

What the game stores

Your progress (solved levels, stars, times, settings and language) is stored only on your device. There are no accounts, we don't ask for your name, email or any other personal data, and none of this is sent to us.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google through AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect from your device: the advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or IDFA on iPhone), IP address, device and system information, and ad interaction and diagnostic data.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before using personalised ads, through Google's consent form. On iPhone, the system asks whether you allow tracking. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad choices. Without consent, ads may be non-personalised or limited.

Legal basis: your consent, for personalised ads; our legitimate interest in funding a free game, for non-personalised ads and fraud prevention.

How Google uses this data is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict processing, to object, and to withdraw consent. Since we keep no data about you on our systems, for ad data you can use the options in the game, on your device (reset or turn off the advertising identifier) and from Google. You can also lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, CNPD (www.cnpd.pt), or your local authority.

Changes
